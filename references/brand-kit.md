# Brand kit

The brand kit is the first design output. It creates a visual vocabulary that can survive beyond one generated image.

## Required decisions

| Area | Decide |
| --- | --- |
| Brand role | What the product helps people do and how it should feel while doing it |
| Mark family | Wordmark, symbol, lockup, app mark, favicon or avatar use |
| Shape language | Geometry, terminals, stroke behavior, corner character |
| Color | Primary, supporting, neutral, status, light surfaces, dark surfaces |
| Typography | Sans-serif family, weights, roles, readable measure |
| Iconography | Stroke or fill, grid, corner treatment, optical size, source |
| Imagery | Subject, crop, contrast, lighting, texture, people, illustration |
| Motion | Pace, energy, entrance, feedback, reduced-motion behavior |
| Voice | Clear, human, specific, confident, calm, playful, or another stated quality |

## Mark tests

Generate or document:

- Primary wordmark or symbol.
- Horizontal and stacked lockups when the product needs them.
- One-color dark and one-color light versions.
- Small-size test at 16, 24, 32, and 48 pixels where relevant.
- App or avatar mark test at 32 and 64 pixels.
- Reversed mark on the intended dark surface.
- Clear-space rule and minimum usable size.
- Fallback when the mark cannot be shown.

Image generation is useful for finding a visual direction. Generated lettering is not a reliable final logo. Redraw accepted marks as vectors or as a precise type-based construction before implementation.

## Brand board contents

The accepted board should show:

1. Mark directions.
2. Light and dark surface samples.
3. Accent and status behavior.
4. Type hierarchy samples.
5. Icon and control character.
6. Image or illustration direction.
7. A small-size legibility test.
8. One representative product surface, only after the foundation is clear.

## Brand quality gate

- The mark is recognizable at small size.
- The mark works in one color.
- The system has a light and dark surface plan.
- Color roles are semantic, not just a list of swatches.
- The type direction remains readable at normal product sizes.
- Image direction can be repeated without depending on one lucky image.
- The kit names what is locked and what is still exploratory.
